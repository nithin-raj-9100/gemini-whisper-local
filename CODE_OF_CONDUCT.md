# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and
abusive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces. Discuss technical decisions on their merits,
assume good faith, and protect users' privacy when sharing logs or reproduction material.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to keep the
community safe and productive.
